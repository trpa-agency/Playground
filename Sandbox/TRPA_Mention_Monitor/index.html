About this data

  • The collector downloads the agendas, minutes, and agenda packets posted by each agency and searches their full text for "Tahoe Regional Planning Agency" and "TRPA." Press items come from news searches and local outlets' feeds.
  • TRPA's own Governing Board and Advisory Planning Commission (APC) meetings are excluded, as is trpa.gov.
  • Recurring boilerplate is ignored, for example meeting locations printed as "Tahoe Regional Planning Agency, 128 Market Street" and "Sources: TRPA" credits on maps.
  • "Upcoming" means the meeting has not happened yet but its agenda is posted, usually three to 10 days ahead. Upcoming meetings are also searched for the department keywords listed in keywords.csv (one row per department and keyword); edit that file and refresh the data to change them.
  • Department view: the default view shows only meetings and press that mention TRPA. Picking a department shows upcoming meetings that match its keywords (even without a TRPA mention), plus past meetings and press items whose TRPA passages mention one of them. Share a department's view with its link (for example ?dept=Transportation).
  • "How it comes up" is worked out from each document's structure: a numbered item on the agenda, public comment (letters, emails, written comments), a staff report or memo, minutes of an earlier meeting, or another attachment. "Named in the item itself" means TRPA is in the agenda item's own title or description. Treat it as a guide and confirm in the document.
  • Order: upcoming meetings are listed soonest first, whether TRPA or a keyword is an agenda item or appears in the staff reports, attachments, or minutes. Mentions only in public comment are flagged "Public comment only" and listed last as lower priority.
  • "Headline match only" press items come from a news search where the article text could not be checked. Open them to confirm.
